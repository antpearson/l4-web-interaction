Title: About SoundWave Festival

Welcome to SoundWave Festival 2025! Join us for three incredible days of music,
community, and unforgettable experiences in the heart of Leeds.

SoundWave Festival brings together the best in electronic, indie, rock, and
alternative music across five amazing stages. From emerging local talent to
internationally acclaimed headliners, our carefully curated lineup offers
something for every music lover.

Set in the beautiful Victoria Park, the festival combines world-class music
with a friendly, inclusive atmosphere. With multiple food vendors, bars, art
installations, and chill-out zones, SoundWave is more than just a music
festival—it's a celebration of culture and creativity.

Whether you're camping for the weekend or joining us for a day, SoundWave
Festival promises an experience you'll never forget. Grab your friends, pack
your festival spirit, and get ready to make memories that will last a lifetime!

What to Bring:
- Valid ID and ticket
- Reusable water bottle
- Sun cream and rain gear
- Comfortable shoes
- Positive vibes!

Getting There:
The festival is easily accessible by public transport. Free shuttle buses run
from Leeds city centre every 30 minutes. Car parking is available but limited—
we recommend using public transport or cycling.

Sustainability:
We're committed to reducing our environmental impact. Please use the recycling
stations, avoid single-use plastics, and help us keep Victoria Park beautiful.
