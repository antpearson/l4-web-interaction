The History of Pizza

Published on January 15, 2025

Pizza has a long and delicious history! While flatbreads with toppings have been eaten for centuries, the modern pizza as we know it originated in Naples, Italy in the 18th century. The famous Margherita pizza was created in 1889 for Queen Margherita of Savoy, featuring the colors of the Italian flag: red tomatoes, white mozzarella, and green basil.

Today, pizza is enjoyed all around the world with countless variations and regional styles, from New York's thin crust to Chicago's deep dish!

Easy Homemade Pizza Recipe

Making pizza at home is easier than you think! Here's a simple recipe to get you started.

Ingredients

  • 500g strong white bread flour
  • 7g sachet dried yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300ml warm water
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Your favorite toppings!

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, yeast, and salt in a large bowl
  2. Add olive oil and warm water, mix into a dough
  3. Knead for 5 minutes until smooth
  4. Leave to rise for 1 hour until doubled in size
  5. Roll out the dough on a floured surface
  6. Add sauce, cheese, and toppings
  7. Bake at 240°C (475°F) for 10-12 minutes
  8. Enjoy your homemade pizza!
